# Demo Script (about 5 minutes)

Use this when you show the demo to Mrs Candice. Read it in your own words.

## Before you start

- Run `npm run present` and open http://localhost:3000 in Chrome. See `docs/PRESENTING.md` for other ways to show it.
- Go to **Manager > Team and settings > Reset demo data** so the sample data is fresh.
- Switch back to **Staff**.

## Step 1: The problem (30 seconds)

"The client pays for 45 hours every 2 weeks. Many departments work for them, so it is hard to prove how many hours we really give. This system records every hour and shows the client the total."

## Step 2: Staff log time (1 minute)

1. Make sure the view is **Staff**, logged in as Sibabalwe Ngandana.
2. Pick the project **Website and booking system** and type of work **Development**.
3. Click **Start timer**.
4. Click **Add 30 minutes** (demo only) so you do not have to wait.
5. Type a note, for example "Built the login page".
6. Click **Stop and submit**.

Say: "Every entry has a project, a note, and an optional proof link. If someone forgets the timer, they can type in the hours, but they must give a reason."

Show the **Forgot to start the timer?** section quickly.

## Step 3: Manager approves (1 minute)

1. Switch to **Manager**.
2. Open **Approvals**. Show the entry you just made.
3. Point out the "Typed in" entry with its reason.
4. Click **Approve** on one, and **Reject** on another (type a reason).

Say: "Only approved hours count. This makes the report trustworthy for the client."

## Step 4: Overview (1 minute)

1. Open **Overview**.
2. Show the four numbers at the top: paid hours, hours used, hours left, awaiting approval.
3. Show **Hours by department** and **Hours per day**.

## Step 5: The client report (1.5 minutes)

1. Switch to **Client**.
2. Click the **left arrow** to go to the last cycle.
3. Read the gold box: "Pillar 5 delivered 52.5 hours. That is 7.5 hours more than the 45 paid hours."
4. Scroll to the **Work log**. Every line is approved work.
5. Show **Download CSV** and **Print or save PDF**.

Say: "This is the proof for the client. It shows the extra value we give."

## Step 6: Ask for feedback

- Is this what you had in mind?
- Should the client see names or only roles?
- Should we connect it to Jibble or Airtable?
